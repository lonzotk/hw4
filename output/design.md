# Campus Customs visual redesign

- **Fonts:** Kept the friendly DM Sans body font and added Playfair Display for expressive shop headings and product names, giving the store a more editorial campus-bookstore feel.
- **Colors:** Strengthened the navy, warm orange, yellow, and pale blue palette with soft gradients and small offset shadows so the brand feels recognizable without becoming loud.
- **Layout:** Added more visual hierarchy, glassy navigation, stronger hero framing, responsive spacing, and mobile-safe chat and footer placement.
- **Motion:** Added entrance transitions, page changes, a gently floating hero illustration, a pulsing sun, reduced-motion support, and small hover/focus feedback. These effects make browsing feel active while respecting accessibility preferences.
- **Cards:** Product cards now have warmer backgrounds, rounded clipping, lift-on-hover behavior, an obvious “VIEW ITEM” cue, and playful offset shadows. This makes the catalogue easier to scan and encourages shoppers to explore details.
- **Chat:** The existing chat panel now has a stronger branded border, animated opening, improved typography, and clearer hover treatment while preserving its real backend connection and product-result cards.

The redesign is intended to keep shoppers oriented, make products feel more memorable, and add small moments of discovery that encourage browsing and purchase without changing product data or backend behavior.
